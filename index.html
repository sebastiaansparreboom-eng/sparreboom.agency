Work

Two projects that show what's possible.

Dokterskeuring.nl needed to reach a specific audience and turn visits into booked appointments. The site didn't exist yet, so I could build it properly from the start.

The audience is older, so clarity came first. We worked out what questions a visitor would have, then built the site around those answers: short paths, large buttons, and a booking flow that works without friction.

I set up the basic SEO and the positioning, and everything was measurable from day one. The site now converts at five percent, tracked since it went live.

Terroir Grand Cru is a Gault&Millau-rated restaurant, and the website needed to match that level.
